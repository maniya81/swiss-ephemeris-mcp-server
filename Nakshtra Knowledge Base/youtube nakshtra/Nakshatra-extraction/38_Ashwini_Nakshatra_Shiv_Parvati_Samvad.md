## 3. Core Conceptual & Philosophical Framework



### A. The 3-Fold Architecture of Nakshatra Study `[00:28]` – `[01:41]`

Deepanshu outlines three distinct layers through which every Nakshatra (starting with the first 9 Nakshatras of the zodiac) is taught in the course:

1. **Basic Quality & Planetary Behavior `[00:33]` – `[00:40]`**: The fundamental nature of the Nakshatra and how each specific planet placed inside it delivers results.

2. ***Karma Vipaka Samhita* Decoding `[00:50]` – `[01:21]`**: Decoding the *Samvad* (dialogue) between **Lord Shiva** and **Maa Parvati**—styled like the *Satyanarayan Katha*—to identify:

   - What exact **curse (*shraap*)** is forming.

   - How that curse connects to the mythological stories associated with Ashwini Nakshatra and plays out in real life.

   - The exact **remedies (*upay*)** prescribed within the *Karma Vipaka Samhita* itself.

3. **Physical Manifestation & Pending Karma `[01:21]` – `[01:41]`**: How these karmic patterns physically manifest in different charts and individuals, and in what specific manner they will cause trouble/suffering (*kis tareeke se tang karne waale hain*). A massive portion of **Pending Karma** is decoded directly via Nakshatras.



### B. Unoccupied Nakshatras Still Operate via Age Activation `[01:41]` – `[02:24]`

- Most astrologers historically assumed that if a person has **no planet** placed in Ashwini (or any given Nakshatra), that Nakshatra does not work for them.

- Deepanshu refutes this: **Even with zero planets in Ashwini, it still functions in your chart.**

- **Mechanism:** Every Nakshatra activates the **5th, 7th, 3rd, and 2nd** positions counted from itself (*apne se paanchva, saatva, teesra, doosra*) at specific ages of life. *(Note: The exact age-activation numbers are promised as upcoming content in the course and do not appear in this edited excerpt.)*



### C. Core Psychology of Ashwini: Attention-Seeking, Spotlight & The Wedding Horse Analogy `[02:34]` – `[04:09]`, `[05:45]` – `[06:46]`

- **Attention-Seeker & Limelight `[02:50]` – `[03:24]`**: Ashwini Nakshatra natives always want to stay in the **limelight** and right at the front (*sabse aage*). They stand in the **spotlight**, driven to display that they "have everything" (*humare paas sab kuch hai*) and that their supremacy/dominance (*prabhutva*) exceeds everyone else's ("there is nobody like me").

- **Subconscious Nature `[05:33]` – `[05:39]`, `[06:19]` – `[06:24]`**: This urge to display/show off is not always conscious bragging; it functions as a deeply wired natural/subconscious tendency inside the house where Ashwini sits.

- **The Horse Rider / *Baraat* Groom Analogy `[05:45]` – `[06:06]`, `[06:25]` – `[06:46]`**:

  - In the house where Ashwini falls, you are a **horse rider** (*ghode pe baithe ho*).

  - **Real-life analogy**: Why does an Indian groom sit on a horse (*baraat waala ghoda*) on his wedding day? So the entire neighborhood (*poore mohalle ko*) knows "this wedding is happening for *this* person," and the whole world comes out just to watch him.

  - **Key Concept**: You are the **"Man of the Moment"** in the exact house occupied by Ashwini. Every native must identify *which horse they are riding*—the horse of spirituality (8th house), home/property (4th house), or profession (10th house)—because that is the domain they are compelled to showcase.

- **The 7th-House Attraction Mechanic (Aries → Libra) `[03:42]` – `[04:09]`**:

  - Ashwini's underlying drive to display power/status is aimed at **attracting the 7th house from it—the sign of Libra** (signifying wife, marriage, partner, or "the other").



### D. The Secret of Navamsha Charana (Pada) & Vargottama: Karmic Backlog vs. Free Will `[08:47]` – `[10:49]`

- Deepanshu challenges standard rote learning: everyone repeats that a **Vargottama Navamsha** or Vargottama planet is "very good," without ever asking *why* or where that goodness actually originates `[08:47]` – `[09:13]`.

- **The *BPHS* Karmic Backlog Secret `[09:22]` – `[10:49]`**:

  - Every house cusp / Ascendant / planet occupies a specific **Charana / Pada (Navamsha division)** within a sign (e.g., 6°50' falls in the 3rd Charana).

  - **Core Principle (citing *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra*)**: The **Charana / Navamsha number** a house or planet occupies indicates **how many lifetimes old (*utne janam puraana*) that karma is**.

    - **2nd Charana** = Karma is **2 lifetimes old**, manifesting in this life `[09:49]` – `[10:01]`.

    - **9th Navamsha** = Carries a **very ancient karmic backlog** (*bahut puraana backlog*); deeply fixed/karmic with **very little Free Will** `[10:29]` – `[10:40]`.

    - **Vargottama** = Zero past-life backlog (*koi backlog nahi hai*). All of its karma belongs to **this very lifetime** (*isi life ka karam hai*), meaning the problem can be fully resolved right here and now. Therefore, the house/planet with **Vargottama carries the MAXIMUM Free Will** in the chart `[10:02]` – `[10:29]`, `[12:02]` – `[12:12]`.



### E. The Law of Repeating Pending Karma `[11:37]` – `[12:12]`, `[18:03]` – `[18:28]`

- Life incidents represent **pending karma getting released**, so one should not panic or fret over them `[11:37]` – `[11:54]`.

- **The Repeating Test Principle `[18:03]` – `[18:15]`**: The most revealing feature of pending karma is that whatever transgression occurred in a past life, **the exact same test (*same test*) returns again in THIS lifetime**, cycling around in different modern formats (*ghoom phir ke alag-alag formats mein*).



### F. Philosophy of Modern Remedy Modification `[13:52]` – `[14:25]`, `[15:19]` – `[15:50]`

- Classical remedies in scriptures (such as "donate a cow") are symbolic carriers of a functional principle—here, **donating a sustainable means of livelihood/resource** (*resource ka daan*).

- Because urban reality has changed (nobody in cities can house a cow), an astrologer must **modify the remedy** (*remedy modify karna*) to preserve its true karmic essence: providing someone with a resource or skill/education through which they can earn a livelihood and sustain their lineage for life.

### 3. *Karma Vipaka Samhita* – Ashwini Pada 2: Destroying a Family & Stealing a Cow `[12:26]` – `[13:47]`

- **Story / Scriptural Reference:** *Karma Vipaka Samhita* – Ashwini Nakshatra, 2nd Charana `[Chapter/Page/Verse not stated]`. Lord Shiva tells Maa Parvati that in a previous life, the native wiped out an entire family (*poore parivaar ko khatam kar diya*), killed father and son (*baap-bete ko maar diya*), seized all their wealth (*poora dhan hadap liya*), and took away their **cow** (*kisi ki gaay le aaya*).

- **Astrological Decoding (Myth → Chart Mapping):**

  - **Taurus Navamsha (2nd Pada)** → Taurus signifies **Family** and **Resources/Wealth**.

  - **Stealing a Cow** → A cow is a domestic animal whose milk is the household's primary means of sustenance/income (*aamdhani*). Stealing a cow maps to **usurping someone's primary livelihood resource or destroying a whole family's means of survival**.

  - **Curse** → Because you destroyed another family's lineage/livelihood, the karmic penalty strikes **your children** (*bacchon par curse* / childlessness — *aapko bacche nahi honge*).



### 4. *Karma Vipaka Samhita* – Ashwini Pada 3: Abortion, Pregnant Woman Murder & Hunting Betrayal `[16:10]` – `[17:44]`

- **Story / Scriptural Reference:** *Karma Vipaka Samhita* – Ashwini Nakshatra, 3rd Charana `[Chapter/Page/Verse not stated]`. Lord Shiva recounts two grave transgressions:

  1. Not allowing children to be born (*paida hi nahi hone diya* — abortion/halting development), killing them while asleep (*sote hue maar diya*), and murdering a **pregnant woman** (*garbhvati stree ki hatya*), resulting in descriptions of various diseases (*yeh rog, woh rog*).

  2. **The Hunting Story (`17:13`–`17:28`):** The native went **hunting** (*shikaar*) with a friend (*mitra*). Driven by **greed** (*lobh*), a quarrel erupted over the **share of the meat** (*maans ke hisse ko leke ladaai*), and that night the native **murdered his friend** (*raat ko dost ki hatya kar di*).

- **Astrological Decoding (Myth → Chart Mapping):**

  - **Gemini Navamsha (3rd Pada / 3rd House energy)** → Gemini is explicitly identified by Deepanshu as **"the sign of abortion"** (halting/preventing development) AND signifies **friends/partners**.

  - **Hunting Together & Fighting Over Meat Share → Murdering the Friend** → Maps directly in modern life to **entering a business partnership, fighting over profit shares (*profit ko leke ladaai*), breaking the partnership, and financially ruining/cheating the partner**.

  - **Curse** → Troubles/suffering falling upon **the native's children** (*bacchon ko problem aayega*) and various ailments.



### 5. *Karma Vipaka Samhita* – Ashwini Pada 4: Deceiving the "Poor Brahmin" `[18:53]` – `[19:50]`

- **Story / Scriptural Reference:** *Karma Vipaka Samhita* – Ashwini Nakshatra, 4th Charana `[Chapter/Page/Verse not stated]`; also references the recurring Puranic trope *"In a town lived a poor Brahmin"* (*ek nagar mein gareeb brahman rehta tha* — noting a Brahmin is never depicted as wealthy in Puranas). Lord Shiva describes cheating Brahmins (*Brahmanon ke saath dhokha*), taking away their property/assets (*sampatti har lena*), and stealing their **knowledge (*Vidya har lena*)**—learning half of it by deceit or forcefully snatching it and killing/harming the Brahmin.

- **Astrological Decoding (Myth → Chart Mapping):**

  - **Cancer Navamsha (4th Pada)** → Cancer is a **Brahmin (Varna) sign** (and relates to the stomach).

  - **What Can You Steal From a Poor Brahmin?** → Not money, because he has none! You steal by: (a) having him perform a **puja/ritual and refusing to pay his *Dakshina***, or (b) **acquiring knowledge (*Vidya*) from a teacher/guru/mentor by deceit** and then harming or abandoning them.

  - **Curse** → **Stomach diseases** (*pet mein bimari*, tied to stealing *Vidya*) and karmic suffering/illness falling upon **your children**.
