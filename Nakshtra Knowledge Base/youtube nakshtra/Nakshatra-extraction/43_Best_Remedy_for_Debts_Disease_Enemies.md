## 3. Core Conceptual & Philosophical Framework



- **Retrograde Planetary Mechanics (`[00:07–00:16]`):**

  - Deepanshu reminds students of a prior foundational rule about retrograde planets: when a planet is retrograde, **"it takes a lot of efforts in that house now."**

  - Consequently, when the 6th lord is retrograde and connected to the *Lagna*, the suffering or disease manifestation intensifies and requires heavy effort to manage.

- **First-Principles Synthesis vs. Rote Memorization (`[02:44–03:10]`):**

  - Deepanshu explicitly rejects memorizing textbook predictive one-liners—even those found in classical texts like *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS)*.

  - Instead, his Lunar Astro methodology relies on synthesizing three atomic building blocks on the fly:

    1. **Planetary *Karaka* (Significator):** What the planet literally represents in the body/life (e.g., Saturn = bones; Jupiter = liver/fat; Venus = kidneys/diabetes; Mars = blood).

    2. **House Signification:** What arena/event the house triggers (e.g., 6th house = disease; 8th house = suddenness, accidents, in-laws, inheritance).

    3. **Nodal Modifiers (Rahu & Ketu):** How the nodes distort the *Karaka* — **Rahu** signifies *enlargement/increase/excess*, while **Ketu** signifies *deficiency/breakage*.

- **Symmetry of the 1st–6th Axis (`[00:48–00:50, 02:35–02:38]`):**

  - A disease theme manifests whether the 6th lord **aspects** the *Lagna*, **occupies** the *Lagna*, or the ***Lagna* lord goes into the 6th house** ("*Lagna* lord in 6th house: same. Theme of life becomes same").

- **Dual-Lordship & Non-Cancellation by Own-Sign (*Swakshetra*) Placement (`[03:32–04:57, 05:35–06:16]`):**

  - Even when a planet is the *Lagna* lord sitting comfortably in its own sign in the *Lagna*, if it simultaneously rules the 6th house (e.g., Venus for Taurus Ascendant) or the 8th house (e.g., Venus for Libra Ascendant; Mars for Aries Ascendant), **it still delivers the malefic disease, disruption, or in-law problems of that *Dusthana* house.**

- **6th House Animal Symbology as a Remedial Lever (`[06:28–08:20]`):**

  - The 6th house governs **animals** in addition to disease. Therefore, channeling charity/food/service toward the specific animal ruled by the 6th-lord planet pacifies the disease associated with that planet.

  - Deepanshu notes this is **"one part of remedy"** among several possible remedial avenues that can be derived from the chart.

- **Pedagogical Warm-Up & "Circuit Completion" Framework (`[03:15–03:26, 08:21–08:50]`):**

  - Deepanshu compares this rapid-fire Q&A warm-up to a preview or **"before the actual act, the clown comes in... just to set up the mood"** before beginning the core *Yogini Dasa* lecture.

  - He stresses that predicting events accurately is impossible without first knowing the underlying planetary combinations (`[08:21–08:29]`).

  - For chart reading practice, he introduces the Lunar Astro analytical prompt: looking at **"which circuit gets completed"** across charts (including D10) before making predictions (`[08:35–08:50]`).

## 6. Master Predictive Rules, Sutras & Formulas



- **Rule 1 `[00:00–00:16, 00:48–00:50]` – 6th Lord Connected to *Lagna* Causes Disease**

  - **Condition (IF):** Any 6th lord aspects the *Lagna* OR sits in the *Lagna*.

  - **Prediction / Manifestation (THEN):** The native will suffer from a disease corresponding to the *Karaka* of that 6th lord.

  - **Modifiers / Exceptions (UNLESS / CHECK ALSO):** Especially pronounced when the 6th lord is **retrograde**.

  - **Mechanism (WHY):** The 6th house/lord represents disease, and retrograde planets force "a lot of efforts" in the house they influence.



- **Rule 2 `[00:17–00:36, 00:48–00:55, 01:07–01:28]` – 6th Lord Jupiter Aspecting or Occupying *Lagna***

  - **Condition (IF):** Jupiter is the 6th lord and aspects the *Lagna* or sits in the *Lagna* (e.g., Cancer Ascendant with Jupiter in 1st house).

  - **Prediction / Manifestation (THEN):**

    1. Hepatitis problem and liver issues.

    2. Fat on the liver (fatty liver) — "very clear prediction."

    3. Chronic indigestion and acidity issues.

    4. Inability to eat three meals a day because the native cannot digest it.

  - **Modifiers / Exceptions (UNLESS / CHECK ALSO):** Does **not** specifically indicate diabetes (diabetes is ruled by Venus, not Jupiter). Check direct vs. retrograde motion for outer body weight (see Rule 3).

  - **Mechanism (WHY):** Jupiter is the natural *Karaka* for the liver, digestion, and fat.



- **Rule 3 `[01:13–01:25]` – Retrograde vs. Direct Jupiter Body-Weight Rule**

  - **Condition (IF):** Jupiter (in the context of 6th-lord Jupiter influencing *Lagna*) is **retrograde** vs. in **direct motion**.
