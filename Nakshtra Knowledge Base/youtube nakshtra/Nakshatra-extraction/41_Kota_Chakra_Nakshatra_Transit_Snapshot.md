## 3. Core Conceptual & Philosophical Framework



- **Etymology & Fortress Analogy of Kota Chakra `[00:57]` – `[01:17]`:**

  - *Kot* (*Kota*) is a Rajasthani word meaning **fort**.

  - Just as a physical fort has concentric defense walls and inner levels protecting the core, the *Kota Chakra* maps Nakshatras onto four concentric levels—from the outermost perimeter (*Bahya*) inward to the central pillar (*Stambha*).

- **Historical & Broad Scope of Application `[01:17]` – `[01:51]`:**

  - Historically, kings commissioned the *Kota Chakra* to determine whether their fort was safe or if imminent danger/problems were approaching the kingdom `[01:32]`.

  - While K.N. Rao wrote an acclaimed book on Kota Chakra applying it strictly to **health** `[01:17]`, Deepanshu teaches that it functions reliably as a daily **snapshot transit technique** `[00:34]`, `[01:26]`.

  - It works across **mundane charts**, **national/country charts**, and **personal natal charts** `[01:43]`.

- **Personalized vs. Generic Transits `[09:47]` – `[10:05]`, `[15:59]` – `[16:15]`:**

  - Deepanshu explicitly dismisses generic transit videos (e.g., *"Saturn moving into X house—what will happen"*) as *"not astrology"* `[16:04]`.

  - True transit analysis must be personalized every single time. Because Kota Chakra anchors the entire 28-Nakshatra grid to the individual's own birth Nakshatra, the exact same planetary transit will be entering one person's fort while exiting another's `[09:47]`.

- **Hierarchy of Timing — Dasha vs. Transit `[10:21]` – `[10:42]`:**

  - **Core Axiom:** *"Transits only deliver what Dasha promises. Whatever Dasha has not promised, transits are not going to deliver it."* `[10:21]`

  - Always evaluate the *Yoga* promised by the active Dasha first before interpreting what transits will trigger `[10:31]`.

- **The Real Worth of Astrology (Pending Karma Philosophy) `[02:41]` – `[03:15]`:**

  - Predicting job, career, or marriage is easy and *"not a big thing"* `[02:43]`.

  - The true worth of astrology lies in **pinpointing the exact past/ancestral karmic event down to the year**—telling a native, *"Your specific ancestor committed this specific act in this year, which is why you are suffering now"*—so they know exactly where corrections must be made `[02:51]`–`[03:13]`.

- **Logic Over Rote Memorization in *Jyotish* `[14:26]` – `[15:44]`:**

  - You cannot learn *Jyotish* by memorizing 300 yogas or lists of traits; you must grasp the underlying **logic**. Once you know the logic, predictions arise automatically without needing to memorize `[14:29]`.

  - Deepanshu teaches spontaneously in flow without PowerPoints or reference notes—arguing that PowerPoints put even the teacher to sleep—and emphasizes going deep into fundamentals (e.g., a 2.5-hour lecture solely on the sign Aries so you know what to predict wherever Aries sits) `[14:42]`–`[15:22]`.

  - *Jyotish* cannot be learned mechanically; *"it has to come through very naturally, through the blood"* `[15:26]`.
