## 4. Mythological Stories & Astrological Decoding



### Story 1: Lord Brahma, the *Manasputras*, and the Origin of Lust `[01:41]`–`[02:42]`

- **Source Cited:** General scriptural reference (*"pehle ke agar aap padhoge..."*) `[Book/Chapter/Page not stated]`.

- **Narrative:**

  - When Lord *Brahma* originally created the universe (*Srishti ki rachna*), he did **not** put lust (*lust*) into human beings.

  - His earliest children were *Manasputras* (mind-born sons)—born directly from his mind (*man ki upaj*): Brahma simply thought of them and his children came into existence. (Deepanshu notes that the concept of *Manushya* / humans emerged in relation to *Manu* and *Shatarupa* `[02:24]`–`[02:27]`).

  - Brahma's first children refused to reproduce, declaring: *"We will not produce further children to carry creation forward."*

  - Brahma felt immense grief (*bahut dukh hua*), realizing his own creations were defying him even though their very purpose was to keep creation running.

  - As a **curse/punishment (*shraap / saza*)**, Brahma declared: *"Since you will not do this, your punishment is that you will now have to produce children just like animals do."* He therefore instilled animal instinct—**lust**—into humans. Deepanshu emphasizes that **lust was originally created exclusively for animals**, not humans.

- **Astrological Decoding (Myth → Chart Mapping):**

  - **Lust = Animal Instinct → Sun taking Animal Form:** Explains the primal, instinctual tension underlying the birth of the *Ashwini Kumars* and why *Surya's* animal-form encounter generates relational friction and untamed heat rather than refined domestic harmony.



### Story 2: *Surya* (Sun) and the Birth of the *Ashwini Kumars* `[01:12]`–`[01:41]`, `[02:43]`–`[03:59]`

- **Source Cited:** Previously narrated in Deepanshu's Nakshatra course `[Purana/Chapter/Page not stated]`.

- **Narrative:**

  - *Surya* (the Sun) possessed intense, unbearable heat (*Surya ki garmi*) that their mother could not withstand/tolerate.

  - *Surya* approached their mother in the **form of an animal** (Deepanshu verbally states: *"jaanwar ki form mein jaate hain, ek animal ki form mein aate hain, mare's [or Mesha/horse] ki form mein jaate hain"* `[02:44]`–`[02:48]`) and impregnated her, resulting in the birth of the **Ashwini Kumars through the nostrils** (`[02:51]`–`[02:54]`).

  - Because of these circumstances, the Ashwini Kumars had a **very bad relationship with their father** (`[01:18]`–`[01:22]`).

- **Astrological Decoding (Myth → Chart Mapping):**

  - **Bad Father Relationship → Ashwini Native's Authority Issues:** Anyone with prominent *Ashwini Nakshatra* struggles in relationships with their **father** or **superiors**.

  - **Mother Unable to Tolerate Sun's Heat → Parental Separation/Strife Before Birth:** When *Ashwini Nakshatra* falls in the **9th House** (father), the father has excessive "heat" (temperament/intensity) that the mother cannot tolerate, manifesting as **separation or an extremely sour relationship between the parents even prior to the native's birth**.



### Story 3: The 12 Adityas (*Surya*) Gifting Children `[02:55]`–`[03:30]`

- **Source Cited:** History of the **12 Adityas** `[Text/Chapter/Page not stated]`.

- **Narrative:**

  - Throughout the entire history of all **12 Adityas**, wherever *Surya* manifested (*jab bhi pragat hue hain*), he gifted **children**—whether he appeared before *Kunti*, a sage's wife, or (as Jasleen adds `[03:16]`) Hanuman's mother (*Anjana*).

  - Deepanshu explains: *Surya* gifts children to everyone he meets simply because **children / lineage are the only gift he inherently possesses to give**.

- **Astrological Decoding (Myth → Chart Mapping):**

  - **Surya = Lineage Expander (*Vansh*):** Sun is the primary karaka for continuing lineage (*Surya aapka vansh badhaata hai*) and physically giving birth to children.

  - **Afflicted Sun → Infertility / Child Not Born:** Because Sun is the giver of biological birth, an afflicted Sun prevents childbirth (`[12:37]`–`[12:40]`).

  - **Specific Sun / Ashwini Placement → Extra/Other Children:** Under a specific solar/Ashwini positioning (tested in a student research assignment), natives end up having **children other than their own primary/legitimate children** (`[04:34]`–`[04:49]`).



### Story 4: Lord Hanuman's Servitude, *Mukti*, and Mars's Lordship of *Patala* `[06:47]`–`[07:26]`

- **Source Cited:** Ramayana motif & *Lal Kitab* `[Chapter/Page not stated]`.

- **Narrative:**

  - Despite being overwhelmingly powerful—capable of single-handedly setting Lanka on fire—**Lord Hanuman** remained strictly in the role of a **servant (*sevak*)**.

  - He understood it was his **Karma** that had to be performed; without discharging it, one cannot move past this realm.

  - While everyone else received liberation (*Mukti*), Hanuman Ji alone was instructed: **"You must stay right here on Earth (*Prithvi*)."**

  - Furthermore, **Mars (*Mangal*)** was granted sovereignty (*swamitva*) over ***Patala* (the underworld)** specifically so that all the **demons (*Rakshasas*)** remain under control.

- **Astrological Decoding (Myth → Chart Mapping):**

  - **Exalted Mars in Capricorn = Hanuman Archetype:** Even though Mars is exalted (immensely strong), being in Capricorn turns the native into a dutiful **servant (*sevak / gulaam*)** bound by karmic duty—especially serving **brothers** (Mars) and family relentlessly.

  - **Mars Ruling *Patala* & Demons → Mars Controls Rahu:** Maps directly to the ***Lal Kitab*** sutra that **Rahu (demonic energy) comes under the control of Mars** (`[07:21]`–`[07:26]`).

## 6. Master Predictive Rules, Sutras & Formulas



- **Rule 1 `[01:28]`–`[01:36]` – Ashwini Nakshatra Authority & Father Friction**

  - **Condition (IF):** *Ashwini Nakshatra* is prominently active/placed in the chart.

  - **Prediction / Manifestation (THEN):** The native's relationship with their father and/or superiors will NOT go well.

  - **Modifiers / Exceptions (UNLESS / CHECK ALSO):** Healing with the father can eventually occur, but only after a significant breakdown and passage of time (`[04:03]`–`[04:10]`, `[04:50]`–`[04:57]`).

  - **Mechanism (WHY):** Derived from the Ashwini Kumars' poor relationship with their father *Surya*, whose intense heat (*garmi*) and animal-form encounter created tension.



- **Rule 2 `[03:39]`–`[03:59]` – Ashwini Nakshatra in the 9th House (Parental Discord Before Birth)**

  - **Condition (IF):** *Ashwini Nakshatra* falls in the 9th House (e.g., Leo Ascendant with Aries in 9th).

  - **Prediction / Manifestation (THEN):** There was a separation—or an intensely sour/strained relationship—between the native's mother and father even *before* the native was born.

  - **Modifiers / Exceptions (UNLESS / CHECK ALSO):** May manifest as emotional/relational sourness rather than legal separation (confirmed in Jasleen's case).

  - **Mechanism (WHY):** The father carries excessive solar "heat" (*father ki heat zyada hai*) that the mother cannot tolerate, mirroring *Surya* and *Sanjna's* separation prior to the Ashwini Kumars' birth.



- **Rule 3 `[04:03]`–`[04:17]`, `[04:50]`–`[04:57]` – Delayed Healing Through the Father (9th House Ashwini)**

  - **Condition (IF):** *Ashwini Nakshatra* is in the 9th House.
