### 1.2 Core Premise & Positioning
- **Why Lagnamsaka Dasa over Vimshottari Dasa on Divisional Charts:**
  - Deepanshu explicitly warns that most astrologers stick to **Vimshottari Dasa** even for divisional charts, *"which gives massive error while making predictions"* `[02:27 – 02:35]`.
  - While planetary (Nakshatra) Dasas operate only on a planet's placement, conjunctions, and aspects, **Rasi Dasas** (*"much more deeper and much more profound once you start using it"*) evaluate an entire sign/house ecosystem—every Rasi either contains planets, has its lord placed in a specific house, and receives Rasi aspects `[04:20 – 04:41]`.
  - **Lagnamsaka Dasa** is positioned as *"the most easiest to apply onto the divisional chart and which does not require a lot of background into the Jaimini astrology or into the Parashari rules and exceptions to calculate the timing of events"* `[03:32 – 03:45]`, and a *"very lightweight, simple, up to the point, accurate system of dasha prediction which you can learn in next 15 minutes"* `[00:13 – 00:22, 21:36 – 21:44]`.
  - However, Vimshottari Dasa is **not** discarded completely: at `[19:53 – 20:06]`, Deepanshu states you can **cross-confirm** the active Lagnamsaka Dasa period in D-9 or D-10 with the running **Vimshottari Dasa** period.
- **Exact Life Events & Divisional Charts Suited For It (`[05:10 – 05:26]`):**
  - **Marriage / First Relationship / Friction / Divorce:** **Navamsha (`D-9`)** `[05:11, 09:08, 21:16 – 21:30]`
  - **Job / Service / Business / Promotion / Hike / Firing / Break in Job:** **Dasamsha (`D-10`)** `[05:13, 13:13, 20:06 – 20:49]`
  - **Children (*Santan*):** **Saptamsha (`D-7`)** `[01:47, 05:14]`
  - **Property:** **Chaturthamsha (`D-4`)** `[05:16]`
  - **Education:** **Siddhamsha (`D-24`)** `[05:18]`
  - **Spiritual Progress:** **Vimshamsha (`D-20`)** `[05:20]`
- **Prerequisites, Reliability Conditions & BTR Sensitivities:**
  - **Prerequisite Skill Level:** Recommended when you have some command over predictive astrology so you immediately recognize how a Rasi behaves, though complete beginners can decode charts using the 5-step framework taught in the video `[04:42 – 04:56]`.
  - **Desh-Kaal-Patra Prerequisite:** You must verify that the native is of practical age for the event (e.g., in 2019, a 14-year-old running a 7th-house D-9 Dasa will likely get a girlfriend/boyfriend rather than get married, whereas 40 years ago marriage at age 13–14 was valid) `[09:16 – 09:38]`.
  - **Birth Time Rectification (BTR) / Lagna Sensitivity Warning:** You **must** be certain the Varga Lagna is accurate before applying Lagnamsaka Dasa because **"if the Lagna changes, then the entire sequence of the Dasha changes"** `[21:06 – 21:14]`. Therefore, beginners should start practicing on **D-9** charts where the birth time is completely certain `[21:15 – 21:22]`.
