## 1. System Configuration (Jagannatha Hora)

* **Dasa Selection:** Navigate to `Dasas` $\rightarrow$ `Rasi Dasas` $\rightarrow$ Right-click and select **`Lagnamsaka dasa`**.
* **Varga Alignment:** Click `Dasa Options` and set the **Varga** dropdown to match the specific divisional chart being analyzed (e.g., `D-9` for marriage/fortune, `D-10` for career).
* **Special D-10 Setting:** When analyzing the Dasamsa (`D-10`), right-click the D-10 chart $\rightarrow$ `Dasamsa (D-10)` $\rightarrow$ select **`Parivritti-dwaya dasamsa (cyclic)`** (`D-10 (Pv)`).

---

## 2. Core Operating Principle

* **Sign-Based (Rasi) Dasa:** Unlike planet-based dashas (like Vimshottari), Lagnamsaka Dasa activates **Zodiac Signs**.
* **Starting Point:** The Dasa sequence always begins from the **Lagna (Ascendant) sign of the selected Divisional Chart**.
* **Standalone Chart Treatment:** Once the Dasa is synced to a specific Varga (e.g., `D-9` or `D-10`), interpret that divisional chart as a standalone chart using house placements, lordships, and aspects within that Varga.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Interpretation Algorithm

For any target timeframe, identify the active **Mahadasha (MD)**, **Antardasha (AD)**, and **Pratyantardasha (PD)** signs, and evaluate each active sign using the following four parameters:

### 1. House Signification (Bhava)
* Count which house the active Dasa sign represents from the **Varga Lagna**.
* **Rule:** The core themes of that house become the primary focus of the period:
  * **6th House:** Conflict, hard work, litigation
  * **7th House:** Marriage, partnerships, public
  * **10th House:** Career peak, fame
  * **11th House:** Gains, awards
  * **12th House:** Foreign lands, cinema, expenses, isolation

### 2. Occupants of the Sign
* Check if any planets are placed inside the active Dasa sign.
* **Rule:** Occupying planets directly deliver results according to their natural significations (*Karaka*) and their house lordships in that Varga (e.g., Venus in the 12th house of `D-10` activates entertainment/cinema).

### 3. Placement & Conjunction of the Sign Lord
* If the Dasa sign is empty (or to find the ultimate outcome of the house), locate the **Lord of the Dasa sign**.
* **Rule:** The house where the lord is posited—and the planets conjunct with it—determine how the event manifests.
* **Worked Example:** In `D-9` (Pisces Lagna), **Leo AD** activates the 6th house (struggle/work). Its lord, **Sun**, sits in the 12th house conjunct **Jupiter** (1st and 10th lord). **Result:** Hard work (6th) leads to massive professional success and recognition (Jupiter as 1st/10th lord).

### 4. Sign Aspects (Rasi Drishti)
* Apply **Jaimini Rasi Drishti** to the active Dasa sign and its lord:
  * **Movable signs** (*Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn*) aspect all **Fixed signs** *except the adjacent one*.
  * **Fixed signs** (*Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius*) aspect all **Movable signs** *except the adjacent one*.
  * **Dual signs** (*Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces*) aspect all other **Dual signs**.
* **Rule:** Planets aspecting the active sign modify the results (benefics bring ease/success; malefics bring disruption/intensity).

## 4. Synthesizing Timeframes (Funnel Approach)

| Level | Scope | Analytical Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mahadasha (MD)** | 7–12 year backdrop | Establishes the overarching life chapter based on the MD sign's house in the Varga. |
| **Antardasha (AD)** | Annual events | Triggers specific annual events within the context of the MD. |
| **Pratyantardasha (PD)** | Exact month / week | Pinpoints the exact month/week of delivery. Look for PD signs containing strong planets or ruling key houses (e.g., 10th house PD for public release/action; 11th house PD for financial payout/recognition). |
