## 2. Engine 1: *Laghu Parashari* Shloka 1 — Nakshatra-Pada Quarter Timing (`[L11]`)

### 2.1 Why Standard KP Fails vs. Deepanshu's 4-Quarter Pada Division
- **Critique of Standard KP Rule**: Krishnamurti Paddhati (KP) asserts that a planet in its dasha *always* delivers the results of its **Nakshatra Lord** across the entire Mahadasha rather than its own or sign results `[L11:L31, L11:L89]`.
- **Deepanshu Giri's *Laghu Parashari* Shloka 1 Rule**: A planet does **NOT** deliver its Nakshatra Lord's results throughout its entire Mahadasha `[L11:L32, L11:L89]`. Instead:
  1. Divide the total Mahadasha duration into **4 equal quarters** corresponding to the **4 Padas (Charanas 1–4)** of the Nakshatra occupied by the Mahadasha lord `[L11:L32-L35, L11:L89, L11:L127]`:
     $$\text{Quarter Duration} = \frac{\text{Total Mahadasha Years}}{4}$$
  2. During the **single quarter matching the planet's natal Nakshatra Pada (1, 2, 3, or 4)**, the Mahadasha lord **transforms into its Nakshatra Lord** and delivers the **Nakshatra Lord's results (and any Nakshatra/Karma Vipaka curse)** `[L11:L32-L41, L11:L89-L97, L11:L127]`.
  3. During the **other three quarters**, the Mahadasha lord delivers **its own results** (unless triggered early by Exception 1 or modified by Exception 2 below) `[L11:L32, L11:L89-L93, L11:L127]`.

## 5. Engine 4: Rashi/Chara Dasha *Bhava Bala* Threshold Formula (`[L12]`)

### 5.1 System Scope & Guardrail
- **Strictly for Rashi (Sign-Based) Dashas**: This mathematical engine applies **ONLY to Rashi Dashas** (such as **K.N. Rao's Chara Dasha** or any sign-based dasha) `[L12:L67, L12:L119, L12:L121, L12:L229]`.
- **Never Use on Nakshatra Dashas**: Do **NOT** apply Bhava Bala thresholds to **Vimshottari Dasha** or other Nakshatra-based dashas, which depend on Nakshatra strength rather than sign Bhava Bala `[L12:L67, L12:L121, L12:L125, L12:L229]`.

### 5.2 Step-by-Step Mathematical Algorithm (`Single-Level` to `6-Level Prana Dasha`)

1. **Step 1 — Compute the Chart Average Bhava Bala ($\bar{B}$)**:
   - Extract the numerical **Bhava Bala** scores of all **12 houses ($h = 1, 2, \dots, 12$)** from your software and divide their sum by $12$ `[L12:L69-L72, L12:L121-L126]`:
     $$\bar{B} = \text{Chart Average Bhava Bala} = \frac{\sum_{h=1}^{12} \text{Bhava Bala}(h)}{12}$$
     *(Typical chart averages cited in class: `300`, `400`, `450`, `500`)* `[L12:L72, L12:L123, L12:L126]`.

2. **Step 2 — Single-Level Evaluation (Mahadasha or Antardasha Alone)**:
   - Let $h_1$ be the natal house corresponding to the active Rashi Dasha sign:
     - If $\text{Bhava Bala}(h_1) > \bar{B} \implies$ **Upward / Favorable Period** `[L12:L73, L12:L123]`.
     - If $\text{Bhava Bala}(h_1) < \bar{B} \implies$ **Downward / Declining Period** `[L12:L73, L12:L123]`.
   - *Worked Class Example*: Chart Average $\bar{B} = 500$; running **Scorpio (6th house)** has $\text{Bhava Bala}(6) = 350 < 500 \implies$ life declines during that dasha `[L12:L73, L12:L123, L12:L314]`.

3. **Step 3 — Multi-Level To-the-Day Evaluation ($N$ Active Dasha Levels)**:
   - To pinpoint exact months, days, or hours across **$N$ simultaneous Rashi-Dasha levels**—e.g., **$N = 3$** (`MD + AD + PD`) or **$N = 6$** (`MD + AD + PD + Sookshma + Prana + Deha`):
     1. Calculate the **$N$-Level Threshold ($T_N$)**:
        $$T_N = N \times \bar{B}$$
        - For $\bar{B} = 500$ and **$N = 3$ levels**: $T_3 = 3 \times 500 = \mathbf{1500}$ `[L12:L75, L12:L123-L125]`.
        - For $\bar{B} = 500$ and **$N = 6$ levels**: $T_6 = 6 \times 500 = \mathbf{3000}$ `[L12:L75, L12:L123-L125]`.
     2. Sum the Bhava Bala scores of the **$N$ natal houses ($h_1, h_2, \dots, h_N$)** corresponding to the $N$ active Rashi-Dasha signs on that day `[L12:L76, L12:L123-L125]`:
        $$S_N = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{Bhava Bala}(h_i)$$
     3. **Compare $S_N$ Against $T_N$**:
        - **$S_N > T_N$ (Sum Exceeds Threshold)**: **Good Period / Favorable Day**—major positive life breakthroughs (such as Deepanshu's exact dates for securing ship jobs) occur when $S_N$ jumps sharply above $T_N$ `[L12:L77, L12:L125, L12:L316]`.
        - **$S_N < T_N$ (Sum Below Threshold)**: **Bad Period / Unfavorable Day** `[L12:L78, L12:L125]`.
   - *Worked 3-Level Class Example*: Running **Sagittarius (7th house) + Aries (11th house) + Virgo (4th house)** $\rightarrow$ compare $\text{Bhava Bala}(7) + \text{Bhava Bala}(11) + \text{Bhava Bala}(4)$ against $3 \times 500 = 1500$ `[L12:L121-L125, L12:L315]`.
