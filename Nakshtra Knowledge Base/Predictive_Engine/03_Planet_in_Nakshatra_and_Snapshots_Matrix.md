### 1.2 The Three-Principle Event Triad (House vs. Lord vs. Karaka)
To resolve apparent contradictions when multiple Nakshatras touch one life domain (e.g., 7th cusp in Krittika Pada 1, 7th lord Mars in Bharani, Venus in Ashwini), separate every domain into three distinct functions `[L04:L73-L77]`:

1. **House / Cusp Nakshatra = Event Occurrence & Rising Persona**: Shows **whether the event will occur** and the default rising nature of that domain/person (activated during the cusp Nakshatra lord's dasha, e.g., 7th cusp in Krittika 1 triggers in Sun dasha) `[L04:L73-L75]`.
2. **House Lord's Nakshatra & Placement = Sustainability & Survival**: Shows **whether the relationship/venture will endure or break** and how crises erupt during the house lord's dasha/antardasha (e.g., 7th lord Mars in Bharani brings Yamaraja-like strictness/bursts during Mars AD, overriding Venus's softness) `[L04:L77, L81]`.
3. **Natural Karaka's Nakshatra = Lived Day-to-Day Quality**: Shows the **experiential quality and daily happiness** inside that domain (e.g., Venus in Ashwini gives playful, youthful, spontaneous travel and cheerfulness between spouses) `[L04:L75]`.

### 1.3 Spouse, Marriage & Family Laws

- **Venus Is the Universal Spouse Karaka (Male & Female Charts)**:
  - **Never** use Jupiter or Mars as the husband karaka in a female chart; **Venus** represents the spouse/partner in **both male and female charts** `[L04:L77-L79]`.
  - *Philosophical basis*: Per the *Bhagavad Gita* (*Purusha–Stree* principle), Lord Krishna is the sole *Purusha* and every soul is *Stree* `[L04:L77]`.
  - *Functional basis*: Venus governs the pleasure, harmony, and marital happiness experienced from a partner regardless of gender (Jupiter was only used historically as natural 9th lord / *Mangalya Sthana* in a patriarchal era) `[L04:L79]`.
- **Married Woman's Family Houses (`2nd` vs. `8th`)**:
  - In a female chart, the **2nd house ALWAYS remains her birth/natal family** even after marriage; the **8th house represents her in-laws' family (*Sasural*)** `[L04:L79]`.
- **Ketu Nakshatra Spouse Law ("Spouse Universally Disliked" — Scoped `[Q4]`)**:
  - When **nearly all prominent planets (~70%+) occupy Ketu Nakshatras** (`Ashwini, Magha, Mula`), or **Ketu sits on/dominates the 1st or 7th house** (plus Venus in a Ketu Nakshatra under heavy Ketu dominance), two signatures manifest `[L12:L171-L173]`:
    1. **Childlike Obstinacy**: Because Ketu rules the 1st Nakshatra (fresh soul entry from above with zero worldly seasoning), the native acts stubbornly and obstinately like an inexperienced child `[L12:L171]`.
    2. **Universally Disliked Spouse**: Even in a love marriage, the native chooses a spouse **whom nobody in the family/circle likes** (lacking looks, job, background, or domestic skill), brought in karmically to force hard life lessons `[L12:L171-L173]`. *(By contrast, standalone **Venus in Ashwini** follows Primary `[Tier 1]` `[L04:L75, L03:L7]`: playful, youthful couple + post-marriage healing.)*
