### 1.9 *Laghu Parashari* Shloka 1: Mahadasha Quarter-Timing (`Q1–Q4`) & Exceptions
- **Rule 1 — 4-Quarter Pada Division (Contra Standard KP)**:
  - A planet in its Mahadasha (or long ~3-year Antardasha; skip short ~6-month ADs) does **not** give its Nakshatra Lord's results across the entire period `[L11:L89, L119-L121]`.
  - Divide $\text{Total Mahadasha Years} \div 4$. The planet delivers its **own results** during three quarters, and switches to its **Nakshatra Lord's results (and activates that Pada's Nakshatra curse)** specifically during the **quarter matching its occupied Pada (`1–4`)** `[L11:L89-L97, L105, L127]`:
    - **Pada 1 → `Q1` (`0%–25%` of MD)**: Immediately at dasha start (e.g., Jupiter 16-yr MD = **Years 0–4**) `[L11:L89]`.
    - **Pada 2 → `Q2` (`25%–50%` of MD)**: After $\frac{1}{4}$ elapsed (e.g., Venus 20-yr MD = **Years 5–10**; Rahu 18-yr MD = **Years 4.5–9**; Ketu 7-yr MD = **Years 1y9m–3y6m**) `[L11:L95-L97, L101-L103, L115-L117]`.
    - **Pada 3 → `Q3` (`50%–75%` of MD)**: After $\frac{1}{2}$ elapsed (e.g., Jupiter 16-yr MD = **Years 8–12**; Venus 20-yr MD = **Years 10–15**) `[L11:L91-L93, L111, L115]`.
    - **Pada 4 → `Q4` (`75%–100%` of MD)**: Final $\frac{1}{4}$ of dasha (e.g., Jupiter 16-yr MD = **Years 12–16**, often the hardest phase if afflicted) `[L11:L89]`.
  - *Own-Nakshatra Purity*: If a planet sits in its **own Nakshatra** (e.g., Venus in Bharani), no substitution occurs (`Venus = Venus`) `[L11:L121]`.
- **Exception 1 — Nakshatra Lord Conjoined With Planet `$X$`**:
  - Even *outside* the active Pada quarter, if the **Nakshatra Lord** is conjoined with another planet `$X$`, then whenever the **Antardasha of `$X$`** runs inside the Mahadasha, the Mahadasha lord immediately switches to **`Nakshatra Lord + X`** `[L11:L91-L99, L101-L105, L111-L113]`.
- **Exception 2 — Degree-Based Pair-Swap for `3, 4, or 5` Conjoined Planets**:
  - Sort conjoined planets in a sign from **lowest degree to highest degree**: **Lowest ↔ Highest swap results**, **2nd-Lowest ↔ 2nd-Highest swap results**, and any **exact middle planet (in an odd-numbered 3- or 5-planet conjunction) stays neutral** and delivers its own results `[L11:L115-L119, L123-L129]`.
