## 1. Blank-Chart Cusp Calculation & House System Protocol

### 1.1 Why Blank-Chart (Ascendant Degree Only, Planets Added Last) Is Mandatory
1. **Nakshatras as Autonomous 220V Power Sources (*Dakshaja*)**:
   - Every Nakshatra is a self-contained energy source (*Dakshaja* = "capable", daughters of Daksha) with its own deity and electrical character (AC, DC, ripple)—like a 220-volt wall outlet `[L03:L179-L181, L04:L29, L04:L165]`.
   - Planets depend on Nakshatras, not vice versa; plugging different appliances (planets) into a 220V outlet only changes the **delivery mechanism**, while the underlying 220V Nakshatra energy operates whether or not a planet sits there `[L03:L179-L181, L04:L29]`.
   - Even if a Nakshatra's planetary ruler is heavily afflicted in D-1 (e.g., Sun afflicted by malefics for Krittika), the Nakshatra itself does **not** become afflicted unless malefics occupy it directly or aspect/pierce it via *Vedha* in Sarvatobhadra Chakra (`SBC`) `[L03:L61, L03:L77, L03:L179-L181]`.
   - Do **not** check the Nakshatra lord's dignity (exaltation/debilitation) or house placement when reading basic Nakshatra cusp effects (`"Less components, easy predictions; more components, more confusions"`) `[L02:L155]`.
2. **Eliminating Planetary Bias in Pending Karma Diagnosis**:
   - Never insert planets at the start of a Nakshatra or Pending Karma reading, because visible planets immediately pull the astrologer's focus toward planetary significations instead of the underlying house-cusp baseline `[L11:L1, L11:L51]`.
   - Construct a **blank 12-house chart** using **only the Ascendant (*Lagna*) degree**, map which Nakshatra and Pada (Charana `1–9`) falls on every house cusp (`H1` through `H12`), and diagnose where the native's baseline pending karma, curses, and behavioral compulsions lie **before** looking at a single planet `[L01:L3, L01:L9-L15, L05:L11, L05:L151-L153, L11:L1, L11:L51]`.
   - Only **after** establishing the 12-house blank-chart cusp baseline should planets be added; any malefic or benefic occupying or aspecting a house/Nakshatra then modulates (amplifies, distorts, or softens) those baseline cusp indications `[L11:L1, L11:L51]`.
3. **Consultation Sequencing ("Rapid-Fire" Protocol)**:
   - Never open a consultation with Nakshatra rapid-fire or sensitive conception/curse predictions `[L01:L59-L61, L03:L15-L17]`.
   - Spend the first 25–30 minutes verifying chart accuracy with core D-1/D-9 predictive techniques, and reserve a **planetless 12-house Nakshatra/Pada sheet** for a non-stop **rapid-fire round in the final 10–20 minutes** without asking the client "yes or no?" `[L03:L15-L17, L03:L167-L169, L03:L233, L04:L107-L109]`.
4. **3-Fold Blank-Chart Overlay Method**:
   - Once the blank-chart house cusps are mapped, layer analysis in three steps `[L04:L21, L04:L27-L29, L05:L11-L15, L05:L151-L153]`:
     1. **House Cusp (*Bhava / Cusp*)**: Read the Nakshatra and Pada rising on each house cusp (`H1–H12`) `[L05:L11, L05:L151-L153]`.
     2. **Planet's Natural *Karakatwa***: Overlay the Nakshatra's traits directly onto the natural significations of any planet sitting in it (ignoring its house placement and lordships for this step) `[L04:L21, L04:L25, L05:L11, L05:L151-L153]`.
     3. **Planet's House Lordships**: Apply the Nakshatra's traits, myths, and *Karma Vipaka* padas to the houses owned by the planet occupying that Nakshatra `[L04:L21, L04:L49, L05:L13, L05:L153]`.

### 1.2 Continuous `1–9` Charana Matching Master Table Across All 12 Signs (`0°00'–30°00'`)

- **Core Rule**: Every `30°` zodiac sign is divided into **9 equal Charanas (Navamsas / Padas) of `3°20'` each** (`9 × 3°20' = 30°00'`) `[L01:L7, L01:L15, L01:L21, L03:L169, L08:L101-L103, L09:L215]`.
- **Sequential Charana Matching Warning**: Match the **sequential `1–9` Charana index within the `30°` sign** of the Ascendant across all 12 houses—**do NOT blindly equate Nakshatra Pada 4 of one Nakshatra to Pada 4 of another Nakshatra** `[L01:L15, L01:L21]`:
  - *Worked Example A*: Ascendant at `11°10'` Leo (**Magha Pada 4**) = **4th Charana** of Leo → 9th house cusp falls at `11°10'` Aries = **4th Charana** of Aries (**Ashwini Pada 4**) `[L01:L13-L15]`.
  - *Worked Example B*: Ascendant in **Purva Phalguni Pada 2** (`16°40'–20°00'` Leo) = Magha (4) + Purva Phalguni (2) = **6th Charana** of Leo → in Aries, take the **6th Charana** = **Bharani Pada 2** (NOT Ashwini Pada 2) `[L01:L21]`.
  - *Worked Example C*: Ascendant in **Bharani Pada 3** (`20°00'–23°20'` Aries) = Ashwini (4) + Bharani (3) = **7th Charana** of Aries → take the **7th Charana** in every house `[L02:L9]`.
  - *Worked Example D*: Ascendant in **Bharani Pada 1** (`13°20'–16°40'` Aries) = **5th Charana** of Aries → take the **5th Charana** in every house `[L02:L185]`.
  - *Worked Example E*: Ascendant at `9°16'` Libra (**Swati Pada 1**) = Chitra (2) + Swati (1) = **3rd Charana** of Libra → 7th house (Aries) = **Ashwini Pada 3** (`3rd Charana`); 8th house (Taurus) = **Krittika Pada 4** (`3rd Charana`) `[L04:L57-L61]`.
  - *Worked Example F*: Ascendant at `29°` Leo (**Uttara Phalguni Pada 1**) = **9th Charana** of Leo → 2nd house (Virgo) = **Chitra Pada 2** (`9th Charana`); 3rd house (Libra) = **Vishakha Pada 3** (`9th Charana`); 9th house (Aries) = **Krittika Pada 1** (`9th Charana`) `[L01:L21, L04:L63-L73]`.
  - *Worked Example G*: Ascendant at `0°00'–3°20'` Scorpio (**Vishakha Pada 4**) = **1st Charana** of Scorpio → 2nd house (Sagittarius) = **Mula Pada 1** (`1st Charana`); 3rd house (Capricorn) = **Uttara Ashadha Pada 2** (`1st Charana`); 4th house (Aquarius) = **Dhanishta Pada 3** (`1st Charana`); 5th house (Pisces) = **Purva Bhadrapada Pada 4** (`1st Charana`); 6th house (Aries) = **Ashwini Pada 1** (`1st Charana`) `[L04:L109]`.
  - *Worked Example H*: Ascendant at `26°43'` Libra (**Vishakha Pada 3**, `9th Charana`) → 7th cusp = `26°43'` Aries (**Krittika Pada 1**); 8th cusp = `26°43'` Taurus (**Mrigashira Pada 2**); 9th cusp = `26°43'` Gemini (**Punarvasu Pada 3**); 10th cusp = `26°43'` Cancer (**Ashlesha Pada 4**) `[L11:L13, L11:L23, L11:L35-L37, L11:L43]`.
- **Fire-Sign (`1, 5, 9`) Navamsa, BPHS Backlog, Yogtara & Vargottama Rules**:
  - **Fire-Sign (`1, 5, 9`) Starting Rule (`A6` Resolved `[L01:L159]`)**: Fire signs **1 (Aries), 5 (Leo), and 9 (Sagittarius)** all begin their Navamsa sequence from **Aries (`1`)** (`Aries 1st Charana = Aries Navamsa = Vargottama`).
  - **BPHS Past-Life Backlog Formula**: The **Charana / Navamsa index (`1st` to `9th`)** rising on a house cusp reveals **how many past births ago** that house's pending karma originated (e.g., `6°50'` = 3rd Navamsa = 3 births old; **9th Navamsa = very deep karmic backlog with minimal free will**) `[L01:L155-L157]`.
  - **Vargottama Free Will**: When a house cusp or planet is **Vargottama** (exact same Rashi in D-1 and D-9), there is **zero past-life backlog**—the karma belongs to **this current lifetime**, granting **maximum free will** `[L01:L155-L161, L08:L111-L117]`.
  - **Ashwini Yogtara, `10°00'` vs. `20°` Aries & Hasta Incompatibility (`[Tier 1]`, `Q3`)**: **`10°00'` Aries** (`P3/P4` boundary) is the **Sun's deep exaltation (*paramochcha*)** and Ashwini's first Yogtara (`10° & 13° Aries ±1°` indicating planned/medically scheduled births `[2024/Vol-2 pp. 80, 85–86]`). **Saturn's classical deep debilitation is `20°` Aries (Bharani Pada 3)** (`2022/Vol-4 p. 20`), whereas `Book P1 pp. 27–28` (`Sa(R) 10°21' Ar`) uses `10°` Ashwini strictly as Saturn's impatient job-quitting friction point. **Ashwini vs. Hasta** is the most incompatible Nakshatra pair (`[Book P2 p. 87]`).
- **Male vs. Non-Male Planet Sign-Bridge Rule**:
  - Only **Male planets (Sun, Mars, Jupiter)** own Nakshatras that divide across two signs (Krittika/Uttara Phalguni/Uttara Ashadha [Sun]; Mrigashira/Chitra/Dhanishta [Mars]; Punarvasu/Vishakha/Purva Bhadrapada [Jupiter]), propelling the native across sign environments `[L06:L1-L3]`.
  - **Female, Neutral, and Shadow planets (Moon, Venus, Mercury, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu)** never cross sign boundaries `[L06:L1-L3]`.

### 1.4 Borderline Ascendant (*Bhava Sandhi*), `15°`/`18°` Cusp Spillover & Distance-from-Cusp Potency Gradient
