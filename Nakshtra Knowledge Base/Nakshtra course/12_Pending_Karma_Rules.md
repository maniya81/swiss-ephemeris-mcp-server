## 2. Exact Calculation & Chart Application Rules

### 2.1 Equal House System for Cusps
- House cusps are calculated using the **Equal House System (30° per house)** — e.g., Lagna at `2° 35'` places the 8th house cusp in **Ashwini** (exact same degree span `2° 35'` in the 8th sign, Aries) [L38].

### 2.2 *Jeeva* Rule (Budha Nadi / Madras Oriental Library)
- **Source**: Taught from **Budha Nadi** (Madras Oriental Library manuscript tradition); speaker notes KP astrology's emphasis on nakshatra-lord results over house placement stems from this Vedic root [L31].
- **Formula**: If **Planet $P$** sits in a **Nakshatra ruled by Planet $J$**, then **$J$ becomes the *Jeeva*** controlling the houses owned by $P$ [L17, L19, L21, L23, L25, L27].
- **Activation**:
  - Even if $J$ has **no direct Rashi lordship or aspectual connection** to the houses owned by $P$, during the **dasha of $J$** (and also $P$'s dasha, e.g., Rahu/Ketu for Rahu in Magha [L27]), $J$ controls and delivers the events of $P$'s owned houses [L23, L25, L27, L35].
  - **Polarity (Good vs. Bad)**: The result depends on the **functional lordship and dignity of $J$ and $P$** (e.g., if $J$ is the 8th lord, it delivers negative/destructive results to $P$'s houses **regardless of whether $J$ sits in a good house like the 4th or 5th**; if $P$ is debilitated, it harms the significations of $P$'s houses) [L21, L34, L35].

### 2.3 Exception: 3 / 6 / 8 / 12 Dusthana Nakshatra & Sign Exchange (*Vipreet* Rule)
- **Source**: **Seshadri Iyer** (*New Techniques of Prediction*) [L35].
- **Rule**: Normally a 6th, 8th, or 12th lord acting as *Jeeva* brings bad results. **Exception**: When **two negatives combine**, they produce **good results (Rajayoga / Vipreet effect)** [L35, L37]:
  - **3, 6, 8, 12 lords** sitting in each other's nakshatras or exchanging nakshatras (e.g., 12th lord in 8th lord's nakshatra, 8th lord in 12th lord's nakshatra, 6th lord in 12th lord's nakshatra, 6th lord in 8th lord's nakshatra, or 6th/12th lord in its own nakshatra) give **good results** [L37].
  - **6th/8th/12th lord in a Dusthana house (6/8/12)** also produces good results [L37].
  - Applies to **both sign-based and nakshatra-based** Vipreet Rajayoga [L37].
  - *Caveat noted*: A student observed that in some test charts 6/8/12 nakshatra exchanges did not uniformly work and suspected an extra catch; Deepanshu did not elaborate the catch in this lecture [L61–L63].

### 2.4 Bhava Bala Snapshot Formula (For Rashi Dashas Only)
- **Scope**: Works **strictly on Rashi Dashas** (such as **K.N. Rao's Chara Dasha** or any sign-based dasha). Does **NOT** work on Nakshatra Dashas (e.g., Vimshottari), which depend on nakshatra strength [L119, L121, L125].
- **Step-by-Step Calculation** [L121–L126]:
  1. Take the **Bhava Bala** values of all **12 houses (Houses 1–12)**.
  2. Compute the **Chart Average**:
     $$\text{Average Bhava Bala} = \frac{\sum_{h=1}^{12} \text{Bhava Bala}(h)}{12}$$
     *(Example values cited: 300, 400, 450, 500)* [L123, L126].
  3. **Single-Dasha Level Check (MD or AD)**: Compare the active sign's house Bhava Bala to the Chart Average. If Average = `500` and running 6th house = `350`, life declines in that period [L123].
  4. **Multi-Level To-the-Day Check ($N$ Dasha Levels, e.g., $N=3$ MD+AD+PD or $N=6$ down to Prana Dasha)**:
     - Compute threshold $= N \times \text{Average Bhava Bala}$ (e.g., $3 \times 500 = 1500$; $6 \times 500 = 3000$) [L123–L125].
     - Sum the Bhava Balas of the **$N$ houses** corresponding to the $N$ active Rashi-dasha signs [L123, L125].
     - **Sum $>$ Threshold** $\rightarrow$ **Good period / day** (major positive life events happen when the sum jumps well above average) [L125].
     - **Sum $<$ Threshold** $\rightarrow$ **Bad period / day** [L125].
- **6th / 8th House Neutral Exception**: If an active dasha runs solely on the **6th or 8th house** across levels and its score is **average/neutral (not very strong)**, that is **good rather than bad** — because an overly strong 6th/8th house activates both extreme positives and extreme negatives of that house strongly [L125].

### 2.5 Superficial Moon Tithi Formula (For Day-to-Day & Fortnightly Events)
- **Concept**: Uses a projected **"Superficial Tithi"** mapped onto the natal chart regardless of where the transiting Moon actually sits in the sky [L129].
- **Degree & House Mapping**:
  - Since the Moon takes ~2.25 days per sign ($30^\circ$), **each house/sign holds 2 Tithis** — approximately **$0^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$** for the first Tithi in that sign and **$15^\circ\text{–}30^\circ$** for the second Tithi [L129].
  - Note the **natal Birth Tithi** (and remaining percentage) at the natal Moon's sign, then count forward (2 Tithis per subsequent sign) to **today's Tithi** to see which **sign, house, $15^\circ$ half ($0^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$ or $15^\circ\text{–}30^\circ$), planets, and house-lordships** are activated today [L129, L131].
  - Because Tithis repeat every lunar fortnight (**15 days**), similar themes/events recur on that Tithi **every 15 days / every month** [L131].
  - **Nakshatra Variant**: This daily snapshot technique also works on a **Nakshatra basis** [L133].

  - If a planet in a 100% Nakshatra Pada sits in a **bad house (e.g., 6th house)** and is duty-bound to give hardship, it initially breaks the native down (job loss, financial wipeout, crisis—acting as a **hard taskmaster**) **to rescue and redirect them onto a higher path** [L5, L7].

## 5. Karma Vipaka Samhita: Stories, Curses & Remedies

### 5.1 *Karma Vipaka Samhita* Dialogues & Stories (Lord Shiva)
1. **Ashwini (5th-House Loophole Story)**: When Indra threatened to cut off the heads of the Ashwini Kumaras, they discovered a **technical loophole** in the condition [L43]. Manifests as natives exploiting **legal/paperwork loopholes** to cheat partners or seize property [L41, L43].
2. **Ashwini Pada 2 (Seizing Wealth by Force)**: Lord Shiva warns that a native who **forcibly seizes another's money/property** (e.g., forcing father/sister out of their rightful share) is struck with **severe, debilitating physical disease** [L53, L59–L60].
3. **Bharani Pada 1 (The Unpaid Loan from a Widowed Brahmin)**: Lord Shiva tells of a *Vaishya* (merchant) who borrowed capital from an **elderly widowed Brahmin** (modern equivalent: someone with **no active income** relying on a **retirement fund / gratuity**) to build his business, and **refused to repay even after becoming successful** — resulting in misery/disease via progeny [L65–L67, L69].
4. **Bharani Pada 3 (The Overloaded Bull)**: Lord Shiva tells of a *Vaishya* merchant buying jaggery (*gud*) at market who **loaded excessive weight onto his bull** without caring whether the animal could survive it; the bull collapsed and died while the merchant felt **zero remorse** [L76]. Manifests as treating spouse, partners, and subordinates purely as profit tools with zero empathy [L76–L77].
5. **Krittika Pada 1 (The Royal Prince Hunting Deer)**: Lord Shiva describes a **handsome, wealthy royal prince (*Rajputra*)** with a beautiful wife and zero financial need who developed a **leisure addiction to hunting and eating deer meat (*hiran ka maans*)**, indifferent to others' suffering [L96–L97, L100–L101]. Manifests as ultra-rich heirs indulging in exotic meats/alcohol/prostitution/bad company and ending without an heir/successor [L101–L107].
