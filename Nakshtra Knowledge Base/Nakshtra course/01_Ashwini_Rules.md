### Four Foundational Mythological Stories & Derived Rules `[Tier 1]`
1. **Birth of the Ashwini Kumaras (Lord Surya & Maa Sanjana)** [L37–L61], `[PPT p. 2]`:
   - *Myth*: **Maa Sanjana** could not endure **Surya's** scorching heat/radiance. After bearing **Yama, Yami, and Manu**, she left her shadow-clone (**Chhaya**, who bore **Shani, Tapti, and the second Manu**) while she performed forest *tapasya*. Discovering Chhaya mistreating Shani, Surya searched the forest, pursued Sanjana—both in **horse form**—and forced intercourse upon her; from her **nostrils**, the twin **Ashwini Kumaras** were born in the jungle [L37–L39], `[PPT p. 2]`.
   - *Derived Rules*: **Breathing/nasal disorders** when Ashwini is afflicted [L39], `[PPT p. 2]`; **7th from Ashwini** = sick partner allowing extra-marital relations [L41–L49], `[PPT p. 2]`; **3rd from Ashwini (`0°–13°20'` Gemini)** = Yama–Yami **incest/taboo ties AND direct Sign of Abortion** [L49–L55], `[PPT p. 2]`; **4th from Ashwini (`0°–13°20'` Cancer)** = **non-consensual sexual molestation** [L53–L61], `[PPT p. 2]`.
2. **Rishi Dadhichi, Indra & Soma Rasa (Horse-Head Surgery)** [L61–L69], `[PPT p. 3]`:
   - *Myth*: **Indra (Jyeshtha)** forbade Dadhichi from sharing the Soma Rasa/Amrita formula under penalty of decapitation. The Ashwini Kumaras exploited a **loophole**: they severed Dadhichi's head, grafted a **horse's head** on him to learn Soma Vidya, let Indra destroy the horse head, and surgically reattached Dadhichi's original head [L61–L65].
   - *Derived Rules*: **Master precision surgeons** [L65]; **5th house from Ashwini (`0°–13°20'` Leo)** carries the curse of **manipulating/cheating via loopholes to extract sacred/restricted knowledge (*Vidya Chori*)**, causing stomach issues and fortune setbacks [L65–L69], `[PPT p. 3]`; **Ashwini and Indra (Jyeshtha) never get along** `[PPT p. 3]`.
3. **Rishi Chyavana & Sukanya (The Pond Identity Test)** [L69–L81], [L171–L173], `[PPT p. 4]`:
   - *Myth*: **Rishi Chyavana got blinded (`आंखें खोनी पड़ी थी`) by Sukanya due to her mistake** (she pierced glowing anthill eyes), and she married him in atonement. Coveting Sukanya, the Ashwini Kumaras cured/rejuvenated Chyavana after bathing in a pond (*sarovar*) with a **game condition** (emerging as three identical youths so she had to identify her husband—attempting to win her through powers and play). She chose Chyavana faithfully [L69–L71], `[PPT p. 4]`.
   - *Derived Rules*: **7th from Ashwini** brings a recurring **relationship loyalty test** when the partner is sick/unfit; honoring marriage heals the partner, while leaving ruins luck [L71–L73], [L171–L173]. Drives **showing off in the Ashwini house to attract its 7th house** [L73–L81].
4. **Restoring Eyesight & Sheep/Goats (+ 2 Other Blindness Stories)** [L81–L83], `[PPT p. 4]`:
   - *Myth*: A man fed all his father's sheep and goats to the Ashwini Kumaras' horse (in ram form) and was cursed with blindness by his father; the Ashwini Kumaras restored his eyesight and livestock [L81–L83] (PPT p. 4 notes **2 other classical stories of the Ashwini Kumaras curing blindness**, reinforcing their eye-healing dominance).
   - *Derived Rules*: Governs **eyesight**, **high-precision eye surgery**, **focus**, and **miraculous recovery** [L81–L83].
