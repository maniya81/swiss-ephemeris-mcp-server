## 2. Core System Methodology (Cross-Lecture Synthesis)

When analyzing any chart using this system, follow these exact rules and guardrails across the 12 sessions:

### 2.1 Chart Setup & Cusp Mapping (Reconciling Lecture Statements)
1. **Blank-Chart First (Planets Last)** (`01`, `05`, `11`): Strip planets initially. Map which Nakshatra / Pada falls on each house cusp from the **Ascendant degree alone**; diagnose pending karma baseline first, then layer planets onto the baseline to see what amplifies/modulates results (Resident Planet > House Pada when both exist in the same house [`04`]).
2. **Continuous Charana Matching (1–9 Navamsas per Sign)** (`01`, `02`, `04`): Find which charana/pada (`1` through `9`) of the rising sign holds the Ascendant degree, and apply that **exact same charana index (`1–9`)** to every house around the chart (e.g., Magha 4 = 4th charana -> Aries 4th charana = Ashwini 4; Purva Phalguni 2 = 6th charana -> Aries 6th charana = Bharani 2). Never confuse a Nakshatra's pada # with a sign's charana #.
3. **House System Rules Across Sessions** *(Preserve All Explicitly Stated Rules)*:
   - **Lectures `01`, `05`, `07`, `12`**: Use **Equal House System (`30°` / *Bhava Madhya*)** in JHora (Ascendant moves 30° every 2 hours; house cusps mirror the Lagna degree/minute across all 12 signs). Explicitly avoid KP / **Placidus** start-cusps unless checking raw planetary/cusp degrees directly (`07 [L85]`).
   - **Lecture `02`, `04`**: Use the quick **Zero-Degree Equal-Pada Cusp shortcut** (matching charana 1–9 without Bhava Chalit).
   - **Lecture `09 [L173]`**: In a Pushya Q&A, speaker confirmed selecting **Sripati Paddhati** in JHora for house cusps is also acceptable in that context.
4. **Software Calibration** (`02`, `05`, `08`, `12`):
   - **Chakra Dasha**: Set JHora year length to **Savana Year** (not solar year) (`02`).
   - **Kalachakra Dasha**: JHora's **S.M. Singh** option aligns well when verified manually (warned that S.M. Singh's printed book contains errors and Saptarishi Desk App has Kalachakra/Varnada Lagna bugs) (`12`).
5. **Dual Planetary Reading (`Karaka` + `Lordship`)** (`04`, `08`): Read every planet twice: (a) strictly as natural *Karaka* while ignoring its house/lordship, and (b) through the houses it owns. Notice Moon is the textbook proxy because Moon transparently absorbs nakshatras; substitute the placed planet's own karakas when another planet sits there (`08`).
6. **Three-Part Event Triad** (`04`): **House/Cusp Nakshatra** = occurrence & nature; **House Lord** = sustainability/longevity; **Natural Karaka** = lived experience quality (*always use **Venus** as spouse karaka for both male and female charts* [`04`]).
7. **System Isolation & 50–50 Filter** (`02`, `03`, `04`): Keep Nakshatra rules strictly separate from **Bhriguvanshi**, **Jaimini**, **Lal Kitab**, and **KP**. Discard any textbook rule that fails 1–3 times in live testing (`03`). Use a planetless Nakshatra rapid-fire sheet only in the **last 10–20 minutes** of a consultation (`03`).

### 2.2 Divisional Charts (`D-1` ↔ `D-9` ↔ `D-60` / `D-6` / `D-3`)
1. **Navamsa (`D-9`) Active From Day 1**: The myth that Navamsa activates only at age 36 is false; `D-9` and `D-60` operate from birth (`09`, `12`). However, **never analyze a child's chart before age 8** (Years 1–4 = Mother; Years 5–8 = Father; native's 9th division activates from Year 9 onward) (`09`).
2. **D-1 = Seed/Action, D-9 = Fruit/Manifestation (`12`)**: Diagnose suffering in `D-9` and trace the causal karma/action in `D-1`. Even a **`1°` shift changing the D-9 sign** alters pending karma (`04`). Check how **`D-9` houses hold each `D-1` Nakshatra's pada signs** (the universal `Rashi#–Navamsa#` code from *Karma Vipaka Samhita*, e.g., `2-5`, `3-7`, `4-8`) (`01`, `06`, `09`, `12`).
3. **Never Compute Nakshatras Inside D-9** (`02`, `09`): Read signs, houses, and planets inside `D-9`—do not evaluate nakshatras of D-9 planets unless backed by classical text proof.
4. **D-1 vs. D-9 Backlog & Vargottama** (`01`): The charana index (`1–9`) shows how many lifetimes old that pending karma is; **Vargottama** = zero past-life backlog (maximum free will in this life).
5. **Hierarchy & Multi-Chart Prohibition** (`03`, `09`, `10`): `D-6 > D-9 > D-1` (must master `D-1` & `D-9` first [`10`]); confirm severe vices like prostitution using **`D-9` (10th with 12/8/2) + `D-60`** before predicting (`02`). **Single-Chart Sufficiency**: Do not open spouse/child charts alongside the native's chart during pending-karma analysis (`03`, `06`).
6. **Three-Timeline Verification** (`05`, `07`, `09`): Verify any karma pattern across (1) **Pre-birth**, (2) **Post-birth**, and (3) **Future/Elders** (cross-checking older relatives representing that house if the native hasn't reached the curse age yet).

### 2.3 Timing Systems & Special Calculation Mechanics
1. ***Laghu Parashari* Shloka 1 — Nakshatra-Pada Quarter Timing (`11`)**:
   - **Rule 1**: A planet does *not* give its Nakshatra lord's results for its entire Mahadasha (contra standard KP). Divide `Total Mahadasha Years / 4`: the planet delivers its **Nakshatra lord's results in the quarter (`1–4`) matching its occupied Pada** (`0–25%`, `25–50%`, `50–75%`, `75–100%`), replacing the MD lord mentally with the NL while reading ADs. Valid across Vimshottari/Ashtottari MDs and long (~3-yr) ADs (skip short ~6-month ADs). No change if planet is in its **own Nakshatra**.
   - **Exception 1 (Nakshatra Lord Conjoined `X`)**: Outside that Pada quarter, whenever **AD of `X`** runs inside MD, MD immediately flips to **`NL + X`**.
   - **Exception 2 (3, 4, or 5 Conjoined Planets Degree-Swap)**: Sort conjoined planets lowest degree to highest: **Lowest ↔ Highest swap**, 2nd-lowest ↔ 2nd-highest swap, and exact **middle planet (when count is odd)** stays neutral giving its own results.
2. **Bhava Bala Snapshot for Rashi/Chara Dashas Only (`12`)**: Compute `Chart Avg = (Sum of all 12 House Bhava Balas) / 12`. For `$N$` active Rashi-dasha levels (`MD+AD+PD`, etc.), sum those `$N$` houses' Bhava Balas vs. `$N \times \text{Chart Avg}$` (`>` = good period/day; `<` = downturn; neutral/average score on **6th/8th house** alone = safe/good). Never apply to Nakshatra dashas.
3. **Superficial Moon Tithi Snapshot (`12`)**: Map 2 Tithis per sign (`0°–15°` and `15°–30°`) starting from Birth Moon's Tithi & remaining % forward to any target day's Tithi to pinpoint the active house, 15° half, and recurring 15-day events.
4. **Kaal Sarp Axis & Vimshottari Dasha Blending (`10`)**: Check planets inside Rahu–Ketu along `1, 2, 3, 4` house order (`144` axis-sign combinations); when Vimshottari fails on trapped planets, **blend Rahu/Ketu into the trapped planet's dasha**.
5. ***Jeeva* Concept (Budha Nadi) & `3/6/8/12` Vipreet Exception (`12`)**: If Planet `$P$` sits in Planet `$J$`'s nakshatra, `$J$` becomes ***Jeeva*** controlling/delivering `$P$`'s houses during `$J$` (and `$P$`) dasha regardless of sign aspects; if both `$P$` and `$J$` are `3/6/8/12` lords exchanging houses or nakshatras (Seshadri Iyer), negative becomes positive (note caveat of an unstated extra condition raised in class).
6. **Budha / Dhruva Nadi Drekkana (`D-3`) Potency (`12`)**: Planets/cusps in designated **Nakshatra Padas = 100%** (e.g., Aries Ashwini 1 defeats `80%` **Parivartana Padas**; `2–3` planets = crorepati; `>= 4` = public fame; `all` = spiritual renunciation).
7. **Orbs, Aspects & Indu Lagna**:
   - **Precise Orb**: Specific afflictions/predictions require **`3°20'` (1 Navamsa)** orb vs. general 6°–10° (`08`, `09`).
   - **Sarvatobhadra Chakra (SBC)**: Always use **all 3 aspect directions** regardless of planetary speed (`02`); **Abhijit** exists *only* inside SBC, never in the 27-nakshatra natal chart (`04`, `10`). Wide Rashi degree aspects lose strength (`05`).
   - **Indu Lagna Wealth Guardrail (`01`, `08`)**: Activate Indu Lagna's nakshatra only if **neither Indu Lagna nor its sign/nakshatra dispositors** occupy or rule **6th, 8th, or 12th houses**.
8. **Three Consultation Prohibitions (`09`, `12`)**:
   - **Never read charts with `Jupiter in 2nd House (D-1 ONLY)`** (*Adle ka Badla* — ancestral Brahmin debt rebounds onto the astrologer within 2–3 days; even mantra japa fails to shield; employee/non-chart advice is safe) (`09`).
   - **Never read charts with `Lone Moon in Lagna`** (refuses to listen; safe only if conjoined with Jupiter or any other planet) (`09`).
   - **Never analyze a child's chart before Age 8** (`09`).
   - **Deliberate ongoing adharma / cheating** (cheating partners, withholding sister's/parent's share, abusive hate-speech) has **no ritual/gemstone remedy** without restitution/behavioral halt (`12`).
