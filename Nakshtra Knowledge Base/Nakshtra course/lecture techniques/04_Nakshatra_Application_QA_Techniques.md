### `[APP-002]` Dual Planetary Reading Method (`Natural Karaka + House Lordship`)
- **Trigger:** Any planet placed in any Nakshatra (`A1-13`).
- **IF → THEN:** Always read every planet in **two independent passes**:
  1. **Natural Karaka Pass:** Temporarily forget house placement and lordships (`Mars` = daily clothes/uniforms/brother; `Saturn` = shoes/brooms/dustbins/servants/CA/IT; `Moon` = mother/home selection; `Mercury` = phones/emails/kids/maternal relatives; `Rahu` = natural `11L` elder brother).

- **Sources:** `[Video @ 00:19:14, 00:23:51]` | **Authority:** Tier 1

### `[APP-003]` Three-Principle Event Framework (`House vs. Lord vs. Karaka`)
- **Trigger:** Analyzing marriage or any major life event where House, Lord, and Karaka sit in different Nakshatras (`A2-13, A2-14`).

  2. **House Lord Nakshatra:** Shows **whether the event sustains/survives** (`e.g., 7L Mars in Bharani` = Yamaraj bursts).
  3. **Natural Karaka Nakshatra:** Shows the **day-to-day lived quality** (`e.g., Venus in Ashwini` = playful married life). *(Never shift early-degree 7H planets to 6H via Bhava Chalit `[A2-28]`.)*
